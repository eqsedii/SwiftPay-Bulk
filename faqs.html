Frequently Asked Questions

How do I buy airtime?

Enter the M-Pesa number that will pay, the number that should receive the airtime, and the amount you want. Tap Pay, then enter your M-Pesa PIN when the prompt appears on your phone.

Is there really a discount?

Yes — purchases come with a discount of up to 15% off the airtime value. The exact rate shown at checkout is the price you pay.

How long does it take to receive airtime?

Airtime is sent automatically within moments of your M-Pesa payment being confirmed.

Can I buy airtime for someone else?

Yes. The "Number to receive airtime" field can be different from your own M-Pesa number.

What if I enter the wrong recipient number?

Please double-check the recipient number before paying. Airtime sent to an incorrect number cannot be reversed once delivered — see our Terms and Conditions.

What if my M-Pesa payment fails or is cancelled?

If the payment doesn't go through, you won't be charged and no airtime will be sent. You're welcome to try again.

Is my M-Pesa PIN safe?

Yes. Your PIN is entered directly into the official Safaricom M-Pesa prompt on your own phone — it is never seen, collected, or stored by Swift Pay Bulk Airtime.

Can I get a refund?

Once airtime has been successfully disbursed to a number, that transaction is final and non-refundable. See our Terms and Conditions for full details.

What is the difference between Swift Pay Bulk Airtime and Collo Data Solutions?

Swift Pay Bulk Airtime is the platform you use to order and pay for airtime. Collo Data Solutions operates its Bingwa Sokoni service.

Who do I contact for help?

Reach us anytime on WhatsApp or call our helpline at 0790017641 — we're available 24/7.
